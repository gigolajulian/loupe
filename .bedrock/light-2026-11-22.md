## Gaps ranked by risk

1. **Exposes data or control**
   - **LOC-1 (medium).** The pipe name `\\.\pipe\loupe-<user>` (Windows) and the socket `/tmp/loupe-<user>.sock` (Mac) are fixed and predictable.
     - Any local process that creates the name first gets every hook event: commands, paths and prompts.
     - With either "Answer on LOUPE" switch on, that process can also answer permission prompts.
     - LOUPE then fails to listen and crashes, because the server has no error handler.
     - Low risk on a single-user PC. No test.
2. **Lets strangers abuse it:** nothing found.
   - Nothing listens on the network.
   - The renderer escapes session text, and the CSP is `default-src 'self'`.
3. **Reliability**
   - **REL-1 (small).** There is no CI, and `npm test` skips two of the three self-test files.
   - **INP-1 (small).** No tests, and two failures go unhandled:
     - `writeFileSync` of config.json at first launch and in every tray click throws in main on a full or read-only disk.
     - The listen error is unhandled.
   - **SEC-5 (small).** The update guard fails closed, and `npm test` covers it. Two gaps:
     - `reply()` turns any unknown choice into allow. Only LOUPE's own renderer sends choices.
     - settings.json and config.json are written in place, not temp file then rename. A backup is taken before the hook fix.
   - **EXIT-6 (small).** A reap PowerShell started just before a crash runs with no time limit.
   - **EXIT-1 (small).** The code ends its children on quit, but no test checks for leftovers.
   - **MEM-3 (small).** Sessions age out after 12 h, but the pipe's per-connection line buffer has no cap.
   - **EXIT-5 (small).** Three things grow:
     - `~/.claude/reap.log` is never trimmed.
     - updater.log is trimmed only at launch.
     - `settings.json.bak-loupe-*` copies are never pruned.
4. **Quality**
   - **SEC-1 (small).** There is no secret scanner. A manual search of the history found no keys.
   - **SEC-3 (small).** The lockfile is committed, but nothing enforces `npm ci`.
   - **DATA-1.** Needs the owner.
